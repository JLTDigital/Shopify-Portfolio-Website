Full stack developer, Shopify specialist

I currently work as a Shopify developer at Visualsoft, a Shopify Premier Partner. Over five years at the agency I've shipped work through the full project cycle — from concept and design through to testing and deployment.

I develop, customise, and maintain Shopify and Shopify Plus stores. That means turning Figma into responsive Liquid, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript; integrating third-party apps; and keeping the storefront fast, stable, and on-brand. I care about performance, conversion, and a customer experience that feels as considered as the design.

Away from theme work I build full stack JavaScript applications. That includes React and Vue on the front end, Node and Express APIs, and MERN-stack apps that run from the database through to the UI. I am comfortable working across the stack when a brief needs more than a storefront.

Shopify Academy verified skill: Liquid Storefronts for Theme Developers — Storefront Series.
